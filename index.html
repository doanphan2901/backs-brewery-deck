Assignment 1c · 400 km for Tier-1 customers

Infeasible: Paris, London and Warsaw lack warehouse capacity within 400 km

1b + Customer Sourcing Policy “MaxSourcingDistance = 400 km” for the 7 customers above 5,000 barrels; all demand must be served.

Why infeasible

Paris can only use Lille (11,400 < 12,979) and Warsaw only Gdansk (6,000 < 7,587). With Lille full, London is 466 short. ≥ 3,632 barrels cannot be served – even with all 12 sites open.

How to fix

Allow ~600 km for Paris and Warsaw (Lyon 501 km, Brno 586 km), add capacity near Paris and Warsaw, or allow partial delivery with a penalty.

Stockholm is not the cause: Guru keeps its only lane (Gothenburg, 508 km).

Assignment 3b · Financial impact and outlook

Covering the peak costs €513k of profit – mostly one-off start-up of the four extra sites

Both models serve 97,730 barrels; 3b must also meet demand in each of the 24 months. Start-up (+€477,504) explains 93 % of the drop.

Future risk

Only 36 barrels headroom at the 2028 peak. With peaks growing ~14.5 % a year, summer 2029 will not fit.

Recommendation

Keep the lean core, add seasonal capacity (flexible or third-party space) before summer and re-run the model with 2029 demand.
